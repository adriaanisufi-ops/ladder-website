LADDER PRIVACY

Last updated 29 October 2026

LADDER is a personal discipline app. It is built to keep your data on your own phone. This page says exactly what that means.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, goals, timers, streak, Mirror answers, affirmations and settings are stored on your device only. There is no account and no login. I do not have a database of your entries and I cannot read them.

What leaves your device

Three optional features send text to a server so an AI can answer:

  • Turning a free text wish into a structured goal.
  • Finding connections in what you wrote.
  • Analyzing patterns in your entries.

Only when you use one of these, the text involved is sent through my server to Anthropic's Claude API and the answer is sent back. I do not store that text. If you never use these features, nothing leaves your device.

What I do not do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics profile of you.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders on your phone itself, such as when a timer ends. They are not sent from a server.

Backups and deleting your data

You can export a backup from the app and keep it wherever you want. Deleting the app deletes its data from your phone. Since I hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

LADDER is not meant for children under 16.
