AI goal builder
Just talk.
Write freely about what you want. What it is, why you want it, when you want it, what you're willing to do. Don't structure it. That's the AI's job. It will turn your words into a complete goal: outcome, daily action, milestones, check-in fields, adjustment rule. You review it before saving.
New goal
Goal name i
Give your goal a short name. This is just for you to recognize it. Keep it simple: one to four words.

Examples:
• Freedom of action
• Graduation internship
• Build the body
Outcome goal i
What exactly do you want to achieve, and by when? Be as specific as possible. A good outcome goal has a number and a date in it.

Examples:
• €10.000 balance by 16 feb 2027
• 3 interviews planned by 1 sept 2026
• Run a 10K in under 50 min by 1 jan 2027
Deadline i
The date by which you want to have achieved your outcome goal. This is shown in the overview so you can always see how much time you have left.

Example: 16 feb 2027
Daily actions i
The things you do every day that move you toward this goal. Add as many as you need. Most goals need just one, some need two or three separate blocks of work.

If an action has a time attached, like "1 hour of calling" or "3 hours on the book", choose Timer and actually time it. A checkbox can't tell you if you did 10 minutes or the full hour. If it's a count or task with no fixed length, like "call 5 organisations" or "make the bed", use Check off.

Example: a money goal might have two actions: "1 hour calling, emailing, LinkedIn" (Timer, 60 min) and "3 hours on the book and site" (Timer, 180 min).
Adjustment rule i
What do you do if your daily actions are consistently done (green) but the outcome still isn't moving? This rule forces you to change your approach instead of repeating what doesn't work.

Examples:
• 2 weeks green but no movement → switch from email to calling, or try a different sector
• 2 weeks green but no sales → change price or target audience
Milestones i
Smaller checkpoints on the way to your big goal. One per line. You can tick them off when you hit them.

Format: what you want to achieve | by when

Examples:
• First paying client | 1 okt 2026
• First monthly user | 1 nov 2026
• €1.000 in the bank | 1 dec 2026
Sunday check-in fields i
This is how you measure progress. Every Sunday you fill these in, and the app keeps the history so you can see if the outcome is actually moving.

Write the things you want to track, separated by commas. Each becomes its own input box.

Examples:
• Balance, Sales this week, Active users
• Reactions received, Interviews planned
• Weight (kg), Workouts this week
