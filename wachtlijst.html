01 / Why it exists

I grew up thinking I was unlucky with the cards I was dealt. That was a ridiculous take.

Time feels fast. Not because it moves fast. Because my expectations outran my actions.

So today, I do one thing. Kept. That is the whole promise. LADDER is where I keep it.

02 / The app

What you get.

Four screens. A day, your goals, an evening check, and your score. Real screens from the app below.

Day

Pick what must exist today. Give it a time. A countdown runs. It exists by then or it does not.

Goals

A goal with a number and a date. The deliverables that get you there. Not a wish list.

03 / The rules

Four rules. No exceptions.

  1. Say it with a number

    A goal without a number is a mood. Get fit is not a goal. Run 10 km by 20 December is.

  2. Break your word. Pay.

    You write your own rules and what breaking them costs. Skip the run and you owe the km.

  3. Stay

    When you want to leave, you tap once and stay. That is the skill. Everything else is admin.

  4. Nobody is watching

    No account. No feed. No one to impress. Your data stays on your phone.

04 / One day

The same loop. Every day.

  1. Morning

    Say your line. Choose what you do today. Set a deliverable and a time.

  2. Work

    Do the deliverable. The clock is on your screen the whole time.

  3. Stillness

    A few minutes with nothing. No phone. You stay, you earn a point.

  4. Evening

    Close the day. What you did, what you did not, one thing it taught you.

  5. Score

    Your streak goes up or it does not. You read the number. You start again tomorrow, same standard.

05 / Who it is for

Be honest. Is this you?

It is for you if

  • You say you will, and then you do not.
  • Your goals have no date and no number.
  • You scroll when you should be working.
  • You want a standard, not a cheerleader.

It is not for you if

  • You want motivation quotes.
  • You want it to be easy.
  • You want followers and a feed.
  • You want to hide from the numbers.
