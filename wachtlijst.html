LADDER
Daily discipline app

Do what you said you would do.

I grew up thinking I was unlucky with the cards I was dealt. That was a ridiculous take.

I like to portray myself as hardworking. But when it is due, I hesitate.

Time feels fast. Not because it moves fast. Because my expectations outran my actions.

So today, I do one thing. Kept. That is the whole promise. LADDER is where I keep it.

How it works

  • Say it with a number.A goal without a number is a mood. Write what will exist, and by when.
  • Break your word. Pay for it.Set your own rules. When you break one, it costs you something real.
  • Stay when you want to leave.One tap walks you through the urge before you give in.
  • Nobody is watching.No account. Your data stays on your phone.

One loop. Every day.

  1. Rung 1MorningSay your line. Choose what you will do today, and when.
  2. Rung 2GoalsWhere you are going, with a date. Then deliverables: things that must exist, each with a number.
  3. Rung 3StillnessA few minutes with no phone. Every time you stay adds one point to your shape.
  4. Rung 4EveningClose the day honestly. Write one thing it taught you.
  5. Rung 5ScoreYour streak and your record. The score does not lie.
