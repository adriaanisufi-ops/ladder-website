How it works

Three things. Every day.

  1. Say it with a number

    Not get fit. Run 10 km by December.

  2. Do it on the clock

    Pick what you do today. Set a time. A countdown shows on your screen.

  3. Check at night

    Did you do it? You see the answer. You start again tomorrow.
