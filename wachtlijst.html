LADDER
Daily discipline app

I grew up thinking I was unlucky.

  1. That is a ridiculous take. The sheer luck I have with my life is incomprehensible.
  2. I like to portray myself as hardworking. But when it is due, I hesitate. I procrastinate as much as I can.
  3. I do not want to get ahead. I want to move forward.
  4. Time feels fast. Not because it moves fast. Because my expectations outran my actions.
  5. So today, I do one thing. Kept. That is the whole promise.

So I built the app

LADDER is one daily loop on your phone: morning, work, evening. You say what you will do, you do it, and the score does not lie. No account. Your data stays on your device. It is not out yet.

One email when the app is ready. Nothing else. See the privacy page.
