LADDER
Daily discipline app

Do what you said you would do.

Every day you either do what you said, or you do not. LADDER keeps the score. Morning, work, evening. No account, no cloud, only your phone. Leave your email and you are first in when it opens.

  1. Rung 1MorningSay your line. Write what you will not give in to. Start one task with a deadline.
  2. Rung 2GoalsAn outcome with a date and daily actions. Timers that keep counting down outside the app.
  3. Rung 3WiringSit with nothing for 3, 5 or 10 minutes. Break a rule and you owe kilometers.
  4. Rung 4MirrorClose the day honestly. One line about what today taught you.
  5. Rung 5LadderYour streak, your wins and your km debt. The score does not lie.

One email when the app is ready. Nothing else. See the privacy page.
