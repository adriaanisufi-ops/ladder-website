LADDER
Daily discipline app

Do what you said you would do.

One email the day the app opens. Nothing else. See the privacy page.

I grew up thinking I was unlucky with the cards I was dealt. That was a ridiculous take.

Time feels fast. Not because it moves fast. Because my expectations outran my actions.

So today, I do one thing. Kept. That is the whole promise. LADDER is where I keep it.

Four rules

  • Say it with a number.A goal without a number is a mood.
  • Break your word. Pay.Your own rules, a real cost.
  • Stay.One tap when you want to leave.
  • Nobody is watching.No account. Your phone only.

One loop. Every day.

  1. MorningSay your line. Choose what you do today.
  2. GoalsA date, and deliverables with a number.
  3. StillnessA few minutes, no phone.
  4. EveningClose the day. One thing it taught you.
  5. ScoreYour streak. It does not lie.
